Report Notes

  • Categorical predictors are one-hot encoded. Each coefficient is reported relative to a reference category, shown in parentheses next to the variable name.
  • Coefficients marked *, **, or *** are statistically significant at the 0.05, 0.01, and 0.001 levels respectively.
  • Binary Logistic Regression: Reports Odds Ratios (OR) with 95% confidence intervals.
  • Multinomial Logistic Regression: Reports Relative Risk Ratios (RRR) with 95% confidence intervals for each outcome category.
  • Ordinal Logistic Regression: Reports Odds Ratios (OR) with 95% confidence intervals under the proportional odds assumption.
  • Model fit is assessed using McFadden pseudo R², AIC, BIC, and likelihood-ratio test.
  • A Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) above 10 for any predictor suggests problematic multicollinearity.
  • Classification metrics are provided for binary and multinomial models where applicable.
